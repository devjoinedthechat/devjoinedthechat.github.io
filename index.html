About

Securing blockchain protocols and shipping payment and trading systems taught me that the happy path is the easy part. The hard part is the retry that charges twice, the message that arrives out of order, and the state that's gone after a restart.

AI agents now run into the same problems. They call real APIs, retry when a step fails, and carry state through tasks that run for hours. It's the area I find most interesting right now, and nobody has clean answers yet, me included.
